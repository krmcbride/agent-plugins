# Exhaust the Design Space

When a UI interaction or architectural decision has no precedent in the codebase, build two or three competing prototypes and compare them side by side before committing. One attempt locks in whatever shape came to mind first.

It also applies to product design decisions where the experience depends on feel, not logic.

The [prototyping](../../prototyping/SKILL.md) skill makes the comparison cheap: throwaway variants behind one switcher, judged by observation.
