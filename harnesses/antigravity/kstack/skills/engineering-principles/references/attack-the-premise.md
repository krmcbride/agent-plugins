# Attack the Premise

When two or more fixes that share one premise have failed the same check, suspect the premise, not the fixes.

- **Write the premise down:** the one sentence every failed fix assumed.
- **Test the premise before the next fix.** Find an observation that would come out differently if the premise were false, and make it. Look at how the failures are distributed, not only at how large they are.
- **Follow the evidence.** If it contradicts the premise, look for what the failures share that the premise did not predict.
- **Remove the condition instead of compensating for it.** If one part always lands on the losing side, change what puts it there.

**Stop:** do not start the next fix before the premise is written down and tested.
